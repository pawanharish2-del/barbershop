Welcome at Barberfurniture

Upgrading, restoring or bringing timeless character to your studio? Take the next step today! We supply artisanal barbershop cabinetry inspired by early twentieth-century craftsmanship that transforms your space into an authentic grooming destination!

Our Classic, Vintage or Industrial collections feature distinct styling details so you can select the exact aesthetic that fits your personality and your barbershop best. Every single unit is built with care from premium, long-lasting materials.

At our Products section you'll find the Classic Line, Vintage Line and our Barberchairs.

Classic Line

Traditional barbershop cabinetry modeled after early 1900s European saloon interiors. Solid sandblasted oak wood finished in natural, antique or dark wenge tones paired with a brown or white marble counter. The Classic Line includes Barbersets, Counters and (display) Cabinets. Explore our Classic Line >here.

Vintage Line

Bring back the golden era with heritage old-school barber workstations. Rich mango wood in a warm vintage brown stain topped with a signature white marble riser section. The Vintage Line features Barbersets, Counters and (display) Cabinets. Explore our Vintage line > here.

Barberchairs

Modeled after iconic early-century grooming chairs. Heavy cast-steel frames paired with plush upholstered leather seating and distinctive vintage tailoring in rich colors. Built to be both a dependable daily workhorse and a centerpiece that gives every client supreme comfort. Explore our Barberchairs >> here.
